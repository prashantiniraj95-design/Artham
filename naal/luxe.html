The idea

Artham means meaning.

It began on paper, with patterns drawn from whatever was already in view: a floor tile, the shadow of a window grill, the grain of an old door. The things we grew up around and never thought to call beautiful.

Artham gives those everyday things a second life, in materials that have never met, so that a small object can take you back to a whole memory.

The founders

Prashantini & Dhiraj

Two people, one habit of seeing.

Prashantini hunts for patterns and the nostalgia hidden in them: the tile underfoot, the shadow of a window grill, the paai rolled up behind the door.

Dhiraj brings the knowledge and the technical hand: the material, the structure, how a mat becomes something you carry.

Naal, our first collection, began with the paai. The mat every home spread out for afternoon naps, unrolled when guests came, then rolled away until the next time. We wanted to carry that feeling out of the house, made alongside the weavers of Pathamadai.
